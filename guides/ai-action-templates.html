AI action templates: the format, with examples

Every AI button in the Timetable Builder is a template: a small piece of data that says where the button goes, what data it may send, what it asks the AI and how the app checks the answer. The seven official actions are templates too. You make your own in Setup, AI actions, with a form, with no code. This guide describes the format for anyone who wants to understand it, write packs by hand or review one before bringing it in.

The AI assistant itself is described in AI-PROVIDERS.md (connecting a service) and AI-PRIVACY.md (what is sent). Nothing in a template can get round the privacy gateway or the app's checks: a template only chooses from what the app allows.

How an action runs

  1. A person presses the button. If the prompt has {{question}}, they are asked what to do first.
  2. The privacy gateway builds the data the template asks for, as codes, fills the placeholders, checks the whole request for names, emails, staff codes and notes, and sends it to the school's chosen AI service.
  3. The answer must match the answer format for the template's kind exactly. If it does not, the AI is asked once more, then the action stops.
  4. The app checks the answer with its own rules. If it fails, the reasons go back to the AI to try again, up to the template's number of attempts (5 at most). Then it stops and nothing changes.
  5. A person sees before and after and chooses Approve, Edit or Reject. Approving is one change, so Undo puts it back. Explanations change nothing.

The fields

Field What it is Rules
name The action's name, in the library and the AI log Required; plain text; up to 60 characters
label The text on the button Required; plain text; up to 28 characters
icon The button's icon One of sparkle wand calendar clock people person room question balance list shield swap
description What it is for, shown on the button and in the review Plain text; up to 200 characters
kind What it does, which decides the answer format and the checks lessonMoves, cover, explain or columnMap (below)
place Where the button goes See Places and placeholders; must suit the kind
scope The data it may send A list from Data; the data its kind needs is always added
prompt What the AI should do, with {{placeholders}} 20 to 4,000 characters; no keys, emails or names
checks Extra checks, on top of the ones its kind always has onlyTarget, progress (below)
maxAttempts How many proposals the AI may make before the action stops 1 to 5
version Goes up by one each time it is saved Set by the app

The app adds the rest itself: the standard instructions (the same for every action, written to work on any AI service) and the answer format for the kind. Custom templates cannot change either. A template can never call itself official, use an official action's id, or switch off the checks its kind needs.

Kinds

Kind What it does Can go on Data always sent Checks always on
lessonMoves Moves lessons (to another day, period, room or staff) and adds new ones Build toolbar, a group, a member of staff, the lesson editor, the open scenario, someone who is off school, lessons Every lesson, day, period, room and person must exist; locked lessons never move; the whole timetable with the proposal in it must break no rule it did not already break
cover Chooses who covers each lesson of an absence Cover, someone who is off cover Everyone chosen must be free, do cover, not be doubled, and stay within the day's cover limit
explain Explains, in a summary and up to 8 points; changes nothing Anywhere except Upload sheets (none) (none: nothing changes)
columnMap Matches a spreadsheet's columns to what the app needs Upload sheets columns (only) Day, period and class matched, each field once, and the sheet must then read cleanly

Optional checks (lessonMoves only):

  • onlyTarget: only the lessons of this group, person or lesson may change. Needs a button on a group, a member of staff, the lesson editor or someone who is off.
  • progress: it must leave fewer rule problems than it found. Needs problems in the data.

Places and placeholders

Placeholders are filled in by the app, as codes, when the button is pressed.

Place (place) Where the button appears Placeholders it fills
build.toolbar Build, on the toolbar (only the two below)
group Auto build, on each group {{group_code}}
absence Cover, next to each person who is off {{staff_code}} {{date}} {{weekday}}
staffTimetable Individual, on a member of staff's timetable {{staff_code}}
lesson Build, in the lesson editor {{lesson_code}}
scenario Scenarios, only while a scenario is open (only the two below)
import Upload sheets, when a lessons sheet is not recognised (only the two below)

Everywhere:

  • {{constraints}}: the school's hard rules and preferences in words. Add it to any action that moves lessons; it helps the AI succeed first time.
  • {{question}}: what the person types when they press the button. On Scenarios it comes from the what-if box; anywhere else the app asks. People, groups and rooms chosen from the list go as codes. A typed name is refused, not sent.

References. In a prompt, @[Name] stands for a person, group, room or subject in your school, and is sent as its code. The builder's list inserts them. They only make sense in your own school, so avoid them in actions you share.

Data

The template chooses what the gateway may send, from this list. Everything is codes: S staff, G groups, R rooms, Su subjects, L lessons, P pupils, N cover needs, C columns. The codes are different for every action.

scope What goes
school Days, periods and their times
subjects Subjects (standard subject names; any other subject as a code)
staff Staff as codes, with roles, subjects they teach, working days and limits
groups Groups as codes, with size, adult ratio and weekly plan
rooms Rooms as codes
lessons Every lesson: group, day, period, subject, staff, room (all codes)
problems Rule problems from the Checks, in codes
absences Who is away on the chosen day and which periods (no reasons)
cover For one day: lessons needing cover and which staff are free for each (Cover only)
columns A spreadsheet's column headings and the kind of values in each, never the values (Upload sheets only)
pupils Pupils as codes and their groups (only when an action truly needs it; record it in your DPIA)

Never sent, whatever the template says: names, emails, the school's own staff codes and pupil numbers, group and room names, lesson titles, absence reasons, notes or anything else typed as free text.

Answer formats

The AI must reply with JSON in exactly this shape. The app checks it, and refuses codes that were not in the data. Days count from 0 (the first school day); periods count teaching periods from 1.

lessonMoves

{ "changes": [ { "lesson": "L12", "day": 2, "period": 3, "room": "R4", "staff": ["S7"] } ],
  "adds":    [ { "group": "G3", "subject": "Su5", "day": 0, "period": 1, "length": 2, "room": "R2", "staff": ["S1"] } ],
  "notes": "Why, in a sentence or two." }

room and staff in a change are optional (leave them out to keep what is there). length 2 is a double.

cover

{ "assignments": [ { "need": "N1", "staff": "S4" } ], "unfilled": ["N3"], "notes": "…" }

explain

{ "summary": "…", "points": ["…", "…"] }

columnMap

{ "columns": [ { "column": "C1", "field": "day" }, { "column": "C2", "field": "period" } ], "notes": "…" }

Fields: day period class subjectCode subject title room staffCodes staff studentCodes students block ignore.

Writing a prompt that works on any AI service

  • Say what to do with the data, in plain words: "Move…", "Choose…", "Explain…".
  • Refer to the data by its names: changes, adds, cover.needs, problems, coversThisTerm.
  • Say what not to do: "Only move {{group_code}}'s lessons", "leave adds empty", "move as few as possible".
  • Add {{constraints}} to anything that moves lessons.
  • Say what to do if it cannot be done: "change nothing and say why in notes".
  • Do not name an AI service or use one service's special wording. The builder warns if you do.
  • Do not type names. A whole name, staff code or email cannot be saved. If a word in your prompt is also part of someone's name in your school (for example "will", "may", "grace"), the builder asks you to confirm it is an ordinary word there. That confirmation is kept with the action in your timetable only, never in a pack.

Versions

Each save is a new version, numbered. The last 10 earlier versions are kept with the timetable, with when they were saved and by whom. In the builder, Earlier versions loads one back into the form; saving it makes it the newest version. Deleting an action deletes its versions; Undo brings them back.

Packs

A pack is a JSON file of actions to share with another school or keep as a backup. Save your actions as a pack saves them all; Share on one action saves just that one.

{
  "format": "tt-ai-actions-1",
  "name": "Example AI actions",
  "exported": "2026-10-28",
  "app": "Timetable Builder 3.2.0-preview.3",
  "actions": [ { "version": 1, "name": "…", "label": "…", "icon": "calendar", "place": "group",
                 "kind": "lessonMoves", "description": "…", "scope": ["school", "lessons"],
                 "prompt": "…", "checks": ["valid", "hardRules", "onlyTarget"], "maxAttempts": 5,
                 "id": "", "official": false } ]
}

A pack holds the current version of each action only: no history, no names of who saved it, no confirmed words and no timetable data.

Bringing one in (Setup, AI actions, Bring in an action pack):

  • Every action goes through the same cleaning as one made in the form. Unknown fields are ignored; text is made plain; the place, kind, data and checks must be ones the app knows and must fit together.
  • An action with a key, an email address, or a name, staff code or email from your timetable in its prompt is not brought in. You are told which and why.
  • An action that says it is official comes in as custom. None can take an official action's id.
  • Actions brought in are switched off until you have looked at them. Open each, choose Try it on practice data, then tick On.
  • Up to 50 actions, and 500 KB, per pack.

Practice data

Preview what it sends builds the request from a made-up school (the demo school) and shows it; nothing is sent. Try it on practice data runs the action on that made-up school: the same gateway, the same checks and the same review, marked Practice run, with nothing to approve. Your timetable is not touched, and the AI never sees it. The prompt's own words are still checked against your real timetable before anything is sent. Practice runs are recorded in the AI log, marked "(practice)".

For developers

The template rules live in src/lib/ai-actions.js (TTAIActions): OFFICIAL, KIND_RULES, PLACE_VARS, PLACEHOLDERS, CHECKS, cleanTemplate, runnable, makePack and readPack. A new kind needs an answer format in SCHEMAS, rules in KIND_RULES, and a checker and review in the app (aiCheckMoves, aiCheckCover, aiCheckColumns). A new place needs an entry in PLACES and PLACE_VARS, and an aiBtns('<place>', context) call where the button should appear. Tests: tests/unit/ai-actions.test.mjs and tests/ai-library.test.cjs.
