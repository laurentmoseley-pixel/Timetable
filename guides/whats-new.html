What's new in the timetable apps

For all staff: the Viewer

  • It opens on Today. At the top: when it was last updated, then Today's changes: cover you are doing, rooms that have moved, and lessons not with you today. The reason someone is away is never shown.
  • Now and Next all day.
    • Before school: your first lesson and when it starts.
    • In a lesson: the subject, then Room, Group and With (the other adults) on their own lines, and a bar showing the minutes left.
    • At break: the break, and what is next.
    • After school: what tomorrow starts with.
  • Find a colleague, room or group with the box on Today: type a name and tap it.
  • Four big tabs: Today, Week, Find and Cover.
  • Cover is easy to spot: a Cover label and a bar down the side, in the day and the week.
  • Display and settings (top right): text size, an easier-to-read font, and high contrast. Your choices are remembered on your device.
  • Your own link: in Display and settings, Copy my link opens the Viewer straight on your timetable. Bookmark it, or add it to your home screen.

For timetablers and SLT: the Builder

  • Home is the first page. To do lists what needs you today, with one button each. Below it are the headline numbers, safety and staffing, and cover so far.
  • New names: Build is now Timetable, and Scenarios is What if. Staff, Students and Rooms are People and rooms. Subject choices, Rest days and on call, Transport and One person are under More. Search (Ctrl K) still finds the old names.
  • Print the whole timetable: a master sheet of every group, member of staff or room, for the whole week. It can be printed by site.
  • Summary of changes for all staff (the bell): one short list of what has changed in the week, to copy into an email or Teams post. No pupils and no reasons for absence.
  • Blocked time: PPA, ECT time, meetings and maintenance as part of a person's week, in Setup or by clicking an empty slot on People and rooms. It is never covered, and nobody is asked to cover in it.
  • Edit on People and rooms: click a lesson on Staff, Students or Rooms to change it there, or an empty slot to add a lesson for just that person or room.
  • Right-click a lesson for quick jobs: Edit, Swap, Change room, Copy, No staff needed, Lock, Delete.
  • Swap or replace a person on one lesson, a class's lessons or all their lessons. Free people come first, and clashes are shown before you confirm.
  • Hover over a lesson to see all of it. Compact or Comfortable sets the size of the grid.
  • An empty slot suggests what the class usually has then, with an adult and room that are free.
  • Cover: one button, Cover this day. Printing and copying are in one menu.
  • Print the meeting pack: staffing load, cover, room use and problems, in one click.
  • New school year (File): makes next year from a copy, moving pupils up a year.
  • Pupil Viewer (Setup): one simple file per pupil, with only their own timetable in it.
  • Take the tour from Home or File, Help.
